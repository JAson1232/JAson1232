# Hi, I'm Iason 👋

**ML / NLP engineer** finishing a BSc in Artificial Intelligence at **Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam**.
I build and ship transformer-based NLP systems — most recently at **Tesla**, where I built a
production anomaly-detection platform that turned free-form customer complaints into structured
engineering signals.

My focus: **efficient fine-tuning of transformers** (the subject of my thesis), **NLP systems**,
and **LLM evaluation**. I care about getting models from a notebook into reliable production.

🎓 BSc AI @ VU Amsterdam (graduating July 2026) · 📍 Amsterdam, NL · 🌍 Open to relocation (EU / UK / US)
